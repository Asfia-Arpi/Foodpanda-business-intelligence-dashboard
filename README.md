## 🎯 Project Overview

This project provides end-to-end business insights for Foodpanda's food delivery services. It tracks high-level financial metrics, customer ordering behavior, restaurant performance, and delivery success rates to help optimize operational decision-making.

---

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **Total Revenue:** $14.34M
* **Total Orders:** 6K
* **Total Customers:** 6K
* **Average Ratings:** 3.01 / 5
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** $2.39K
* **Total Partner Restaurants:** 5 (Pizza Hut, KFC, McDonald's, Burger King, Subway)

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* **Revenue Trends:** Revenue trends tracked across 2024 and 2025, capturing seasonal fluctuations and monthly spikes.
* **Customer Demographics:** Insights segmented by age groups—**Teenagers** lead with 718 orders, followed closely by **Seniors** (680) and **Adults** (662).
* **Popular Food Categories:** **Italian** (439 orders) and **Continental** (422 orders) are the most ordered food categories.
* **Top 5 Dishes:** **Pasta** (443) and **Sandwiches** (426) are the top-performing individual items.
* **Restaurant Revenue Leaderboard:** **Pizza Hut** ($3.00M) and **Subway** ($3.00M) generate the highest total revenue among the 5 partner chains.
* **Payment Method Distribution:** Balanced usage across options—**Wallet (33.98%)**, **Cash (33.37%)**, and **Card (32.65%)**.
* **Order Frequency:** Heavy concentration of repeat users with **39–40 orders** per customer segment.
